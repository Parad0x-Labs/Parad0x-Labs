## VOOL: your machine, your agent

[**VOOL**](https://github.com/Parad0x-Labs/vool) is a free, MIT-licensed personal agent that runs on your computer. It reads your projects, edits files, runs tests, researches the web and remembers what matters for next time. Start on a local model with no account or cloud key, or bring your own provider.

- **Remembers more, spends less.** On 150 never-used LoCoMo questions it answered 82.7% right against 71.3% for OpenClaw and 62.7% for Hermes, with 3.5 to 5.4 times fewer tokens. [Every run, including the weak spots](https://github.com/Parad0x-Labs/vool/blob/main/docs/benchmarks/README.md)
- **You stay in charge.** Workspace scopes, approval modes and spending limits decide what it may do. Every tool call is recorded, with signed receipts you can verify offline.
- **Updates itself.** A notice and one press, and the old version comes back if an update fails.
- **Download 0.7.7 beta** for Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux from the [latest release](https://github.com/Parad0x-Labs/vool/releases/latest).
